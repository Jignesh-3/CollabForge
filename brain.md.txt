# brain.md — Project Blueprint: CollabForge

> **Mission:** Eliminate the "solo builder penalty" by pairing independent developers, designers, and creators into high-velocity hackathon and side-project squads through structured capability slots, live event countdowns, and complementary skill matching.

---

## 1. Product Vision & Problem Space

### The Core Frustrations
1. **The Hackathon Drop-Out Rate:** Capable backend engineers and UI designers skip major hackathons (Devpost, ETHGlobal, HackIndia, MLH) because they lack a balanced squad before registration cutoffs.
2. **Discord / Slack Noise:** Existing matchmaking relies on unstructured channels where portfolios get buried under chat spam.
3. **Asymmetric Commitment:** Solo devs group up casually, only for teammates to disappear due to lack of verified skills or clear role boundaries.

### The CollabForge Solution
* **Slot-Based Squad Architecture:** Teams are structured systems with fixed, unfilled capability slots (e.g., `1/1 Lead Backend`, `0/1 Frontend/WebGL`, `0/1 Product Designer`).
* **Live Hackathon Radar:** Direct synchronization with upcoming global and local hackathons, featuring registration countdowns and event-targeted squads.
* **Match Score Engine:** Automatic calculation pairing users to squads where their verified skills fill open gaps.

## 3. Visual Identity & Design Tokens (Crimson Obsidian Palette)

Built on the **60-30-10 rule** (60% Pitch Black, 30% Crisp White/Zinc, 10% Crimson Red accents).

| Token Name | Hex Code | Tailwind Equivalent | Purpose & Layer |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Canvas Background** | `#050507` | `bg-[#050507]` | Deepest viewport canvas |
| **Surface Card** | `#0e0e12` | `bg-[#0e0e12]` | Squad cards, dialogs, sidebars |
| **Surface Hover** | `#14141a` | `bg-[#14141a]` | Interactive card hover states |
| **Text Primary** | `#ffffff` | `text-white` | Hero titles, squad names, key values |

| **Filled Role Slot** | `rgba(24,24,27,0.8)` | `border border-zinc-800 bg-zinc-900` | Confirmed team members |

## 4. Typography Hierarchy

* **Primary Sans (UI & Headings):** `Inter` (Weights: 400 Regular, 600 SemiBold, 700 Bold). Used for brand titles, navigation, descriptions, and user pitches.
* **Technical Monospace (Data & Tags):** `JetBrains Mono` (Weights: 400 Regular, 500 Medium). Used for tech stack badges, role slots (`[0/1 FRONTEND]`), timestamps, and countdown timers. Always styled uppercase with wide letter-spacing (`tracking-wider`).

---

## 5. Technical Stack & Architecture

* **Frontend:** HTML5, Tailwind CSS, Vanilla JavaScript (lightweight, zero unnecessary GPU animation bloat).
* **Backend:** FastAPI (Python 3.11+) with async SQLAlchemy ORM.
* **Database:** PostgreSQL (normalized schema handling users, profiles, hackathons, squads, roles, and jo)
